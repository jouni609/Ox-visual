# Leaderboard

The nine sets that survived the curation of 2026-09-04, the manual re-grade that followed it, the second audit of 2026-09-11, and the maintainer's manual audit that closed the same day. The ox gate: every design is judged on its rendered page by one question — *does the image portray a bovine in any form, and does it resemble one?* — and a set passes at 4 of 5 designs or better. Each set also carries a quality score out of 100, judged on ox legibility, distinctness of the five directions, craft, copy, and accessibility. Scores and gates below are from the 2026-09-11 re-audit, which re-rendered every design at desktop and mobile widths and re-checked every gate-deciding page by eye; the original review's scorecard lives in [SCORECARD.md](SCORECARD.md) and every removal — fifteen sets so far — in [WALLOFSHAME.md](WALLOFSHAME.md).
