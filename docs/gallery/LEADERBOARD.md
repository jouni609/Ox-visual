# Leaderboard

The fourteen sets that survived the curation of 2026-09-04, the manual re-grade that followed it, the second audit of 2026-09-11, the maintainer's manual audit that closed the same day, and the third audit of 2026-10-01. The ox gate: every design is judged on its rendered page by one question — *does the image portray a bovine in any form, and does it resemble one?* — and a set passes at 4 of 5 designs or better. Each set also carries a quality score out of 100, judged on ox legibility, distinctness of the five directions, craft, copy, and accessibility. The first nine rows keep their 2026-09-11 scores; the five rows graded by the 2026-10-01 audit (ox-climates, ox-span, ox-meridian, ox-tale, ox-apparatus) were rendered at desktop and mobile widths, swept for console errors and overflow, and judged by the same method — 0 errors, 0 overflow across all 25 new designs. The original review's scorecard lives in [SCORECARD.md](SCORECARD.md) and every removal — fifteen sets so far, none in 2026-10-01 — in [WALLOFSHAME.md](WALLOFSHAME.md).
